# Sources and confidence levels (checked November 2026)

Official verified facts:

- https://www.opengrid.world/guides/quickstart/ : Lite and Full versions, same 28 mm pitch, tile sizing and wall assembly process.
- https://www.opengrid.world/guides/modeling/ : Bare snap modeling approach; Lite snap usable with Full and Lite panels; original snap geometry required.
- https://www.opengrid.world/guides/snaps/ : Directional and lock snaps, Multiconnect, HSW compatibility notes.
- https://www.opengrid.world/guides/quickstart/underware/ : Underware cable accessory system.
- https://github.com/nredd/fusion-opengrid : Independent Fusion 360 reference, thicknesses and cell dimensional specification. Its licensing should be checked before reusing code.

Further sources for imports, not bundled:

- https://www.printables.com/model/1214361-opengrid-walldesk-mounting-framework-and-ecosystem
- https://github.com/mitufy/opengrid-projects
- https://www.printables.com/model/1559478-openconnect-opengrids-own-connector-system

**CAD validation:** The locally generated examples export to STEP and STL and pass software solid-validity and mesh-watertight checks. **Physical tests:** not performed. **Native mounting claims:** not made.
